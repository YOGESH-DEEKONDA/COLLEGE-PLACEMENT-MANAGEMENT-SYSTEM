About PlacementHub

Your one-stop platform for discovering placement opportunities and building your career.

Our Mission

PlacementHub aims to make the college placement process easier for students by bringing companies, opportunities and applications together in one place.

For Students

Students can explore companies, check eligibility, apply for drives and track their placement applications.

For Companies

Companies can connect with eligible students and manage their recruitment opportunities efficiently.
